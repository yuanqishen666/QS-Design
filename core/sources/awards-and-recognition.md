# 奖项与认可来源

奖项用于发现值得进一步核实的产品、团队与机制。正式引用前遵守 [证据规则](../references/evidence-policy.md)，不要把奖项标签直接写成质量结论。

## A 级：平台与交互一手入口

- [Apple Design Awards](https://developer.apple.com/design/awards/)：Apple 平台，按 Delight and Fun、Inclusivity、Innovation、Interaction、Social Impact、Visuals and Graphics 等类别发现产品；继续追踪产品页和 Behind the Design。
- [Google Play Best of](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-play/best-apps-games-2025/)：Android/Play 生态年度入口；属于平台编辑选择，不是纯 UI 评审。
- [IxDA Interaction Awards](https://ixda.org/initiatives/interaction-awards/) 与 [Entries Archive](https://awards.ixda.org/entries/)：交互、服务、产品与实验项目；标注概念/上线状态。

## B 级：有案例材料的评审入口

- [UX Design Awards](https://ux-design-awards.com/winners)
- [Core77 Interaction](https://designawards.core77.com/interaction/)
- [D&AD Digital Experience Design](https://www.dandad.org/awards/d-ad-awards/categories/digital-experience-design)
- [GOOD DESIGN AWARD / G Mark](https://www.g-mark.org/en/learn/gda/overview)

## C 级：发现入口

- [iF Design Award](https://ifdesign.com/en/if-magazine/newsroom/if-design-award-2025-the-winners)
- [Red Dot Apps](https://www.red-dot.org/apps)
- [Webby Apps & Software UX](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/apps-software-immersive/app-features/best-user-experience)
- [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/)

## 维护

机器可读条目位于 `core/data/award-sources.json`。运行 `node scripts/source-check.mjs` 检查重复、字段和 URL 结构；需要更新年度时修改数据，不在 skill 入口堆叠获奖名单。
